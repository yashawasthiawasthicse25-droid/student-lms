Frequently Asked Questions

Are all course lessons self-paced? ▼
Yes! Every module is completely self-paced. You can mark lessons as complete whenever you finish reviewing them, and your progress percentage will persist across all sessions.
How does the quiz grading work? ▼
Quizzes are graded instantaneously upon submission. You will see which questions you answered correctly, along with rationales and explanations for every concept. A score of 70% or higher is considered passing.
How do instructors manage courses? ▼
Faculty members can sign in with instructor accounts to view real-time student completion rates, average quiz scores, and author new courses directly via the Teacher Dashboard.
